Funding a Tacoma event in 2027

Tacoma keeps its own lodging tax and runs no grant round for it, and Tacoma events are excluded from Pierce County's $5.3M lodging tax program. Pick an event date and this page shows which city, county, utility, tribal and state programs can still be applied for before that date, which ones already closed for it and roll to the following cycle, and the permit deadlines counted back from the date.

How to read eligibility. For-profit OK means the program text admits businesses producing not-for-profit public events. Nonprofit sponsor accepted means a for-profit producer can apply through a 501(c)(3) fiscal sponsor or umbrella organization under the program's own rules. Nonprofit must apply means the 501(c) organization has to be the applicant and contract holder (and the program either forbids fiscal sponsorship or does not mention it), so a nonprofit partner would need to own the application and the event.

Posted dates were checked on November 2, 2026 against each program's official page; expected dates are projected from the last two or three cycles and are marked as such. Reimbursement-based programs pay after the event, so plan cash flow accordingly. Report a correction or a missing program through the Tacoma card.

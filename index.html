Washington Lodging Tax Grants

The 50 cities and counties with the most hotel tax money: who runs an LTAC grant program, when applications are due, whether applicants present to the committee, and who sits on it.

Compiled from official city and county LTAC pages, application packets, agendas and minutes as of October 29, 2026. Deadlines marked "est." are projected from the last published date and need confirming with staff before you rely on them. Committee rosters change with appointments; the date next to each roster is the date of the source it came from. Member contact details are shown only where the jurisdiction publishes them. Under RCW 67.28.1817 every LTAC has at least five members: an elected official as chair, at least two representatives of businesses that collect the tax, and at least two representatives of activities the tax funds.
